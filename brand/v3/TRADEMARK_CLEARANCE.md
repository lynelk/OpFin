# OpFin Trademark and Name Clearance

Status: **NOT CLEARED — preliminary screening only**  
Updated: 24 September 2026

## Purpose

This document prevents a brand-design decision from being mistaken for legal trademark clearance.

## Preliminary finding

The name **OpFin** has existing commercial use outside the current OpFin product. Public material associated with Razorpay has historically/currently referred to payroll software as Opfin/RazorpayX Payroll. That does not by itself determine registrability or infringement in Uganda or any other market, but it means a professional similarity and class analysis is required.

A lack of ordinary Web-search results for a jurisdiction is not a clean trademark search.

## Required official searches

Before v3.0 freeze, record searches for:
- **Uganda:** URSB trademark register/search;
- **regional:** ARIPO IP Digital Library / applicable ARIPO filing route;
- **international:** WIPO Global Brand Database and Madrid records;
- **Kenya:** KIPI;
- **Tanzania:** BRELA;
- **Rwanda:** RDB;
- each additional intended launch/expansion market through its official register or qualified local counsel.

Search:
- OPFIN;
- OP FIN;
- close phonetic/spelling variants;
- the device/monogram where appropriate;
- relevant owners in financial, software, payroll, lending and related services.

## Classes for counsel/search planning

At minimum, assess the Nice classes relevant to:
- software/mobile applications;
- financial and monetary services;
- technology/SaaS services.

Also assess any additional class required by the actual employer, education, programme, data or business-services proposition. Final class selection is a legal filing decision, not a branding guess.

## Required disposition

For each market, record:
- register searched;
- search date;
- exact terms/classes;
- potentially conflicting marks;
- owner/status;
- legal assessment;
- filing decision;
- application/registration reference where filed;
- restrictions or coexistence considerations.

## Freeze rule

Do not change this status to CLEARED because:
- a domain is available;
- a company name is available;
- a Google search looks quiet;
- an app-store listing exists.

Brand System v3.0 can be frozen only when the legal owner has an evidence-backed decision on use/filing in the launch and intended expansion markets.
