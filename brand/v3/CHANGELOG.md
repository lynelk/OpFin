# OpFin Brand System changelog

Language: English (United Kingdom)

Record every brand-system change here, newest first. Never silently overwrite a superseded identity: retire it with a date and a pointer to where its record is kept.

## 3.0.0-rc.1: toolkit additions, 4 October 2026 (issue #103)

### Added

- **Toolkit exports** under `exports/`, generated by `scripts/prepare-brand-toolkit.mjs` from the path-only vector masters. Every file's SHA-256 and source master are recorded in `exports/EXPORT_MANIFEST.json`, and CI rejects changed, stale or unlisted exports.
  - Monogram in primary, reverse, mono-dark and mono-light, as SVG and as transparent PNG from 64 to 2048 pixels.
  - App icon at 120, 152, 180 (Apple touch), 192, 512 and 1024 pixels.
  - Favicons at 16, 32 and 48 pixels, plus `favicon.ico`.
  - Profile images at 400 and 800 pixels.
  - The Progress Path motif at 720 and 1440 pixels.
- **Templates:** A4 letterhead, A4 product one-pager with a mandatory costs-and-risks panel, 1080×1920 vertical story, 1200×630 link preview, and a text-first email signature.
- **Distribution:** `DISTRIBUTION.md` and `scripts/package-brand.sh`, which builds the versioned package from committed sources only.

### Found, pending before freeze

- The wordmark and both lock-ups set "OpFin" as live Inter `<text>`, not outlined paths. Where Inter is not installed they render in a fallback font, usually Arial, so they are not yet true vector masters. Outline them from the licensed Inter SemiBold before the v3.0 freeze. Their exports are withheld until then.

### Resolved

- `docs/BRAND_IMPLEMENTATION.md` no longer presents one fixed navigation menu as universal; channel-specific navigation and activation state are described in product documentation.

### Unchanged

- Vector masters, tokens, platform assets and app-store icons.

## 3.0.0-rc.1: 24 September 2026

Merged to `main` through PR #110.

### Added

- The v3 vector monogram, wordmark, lock-ups, app-icon master and Progress Path.
- The Financial Compass and Next Step product language.
- The brand standard, starter templates and change policy.
- The screenshot capture standard and the visual QA checklist.
- A preliminary trademark-risk record, with status NOT CLEARED.
- CI provenance checks.

### Retired

- The teal and navy Web foundation and Arial-first typography were removed from active Web styles on 24 September 2026 (commit `67c1f29a`). The retired values were `#0F766E`, `#115E59`, `#0B1F3A`, `#DFF7F2`, `#F4F8F7`, `#D7E1DF` and "Arial, Helvetica". `scripts/verify-security-controls.py` now rejects them in active style sheets.
