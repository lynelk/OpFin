# OpFin Brand System v3

Status: Release candidate pending external evidence gates  
Version: 3.0.0-rc.1  
Updated: 24 September 2026  
Language: English (United Kingdom)

This directory is the controlled source for the OpFin Brand System v3 release candidate.

## Fixed direction

OpFin retains its existing monogram and the approved Indigo, Apricot and Warm Ivory direction. The brand promise remains **Your next step, clearer.**

The v3 system makes that direction operational across product, communications, distribution and partner use. It does not change financial permissions, provider activation, lending controls, regulatory status or product availability.

## Contents

- `OPFIN_BRAND_SYSTEM_V3.md` — master brand and communications standard.
- `BRAND_CHANGE_POLICY.md` — governance and change-control rules.
- `TRADEMARK_CLEARANCE.md` — preliminary name-risk review and formal clearance gates.
- `VISUAL_QA_CHECKLIST.md` — mobile, Web, print, reverse-background and accessibility acceptance.
- `SCREENSHOT_CAPTURE_STANDARD.md` — release-candidate screenshot evidence standard.
- `assets/` — vector master symbol, lock-ups, wordmark, app icon, progress-path motif and Play feature-graphic master.
- `templates/` — controlled starter artwork for presentations, documents, social and partner co-branding.

## Release status

The source-controlled visual system is ready for engineering review. It must not be called the frozen v3.0 release until all of these are recorded:

1. exact signed Android release-candidate screenshots from tested devices;
2. completed official trademark/name searches and legal disposition for launch/expansion markets;
3. representative mobile, desktop, print, reverse-background and accessibility visual sign-off;
4. generated production assets reviewed against the vector masters;
5. Google Play listing and graphics actually updated in Play Console.

Until then, use **OpFin Brand System 3.0.0-rc.1**. This avoids the ancient corporate ritual of declaring something complete because the PDF has a date on it.
