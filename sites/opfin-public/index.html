COMMON QUESTIONS

Useful answers, without the usual fintech fog machine.

Is OpFin only a lending app?

No. OpFin is being built around a broader financial picture: managing money, responsible finance, savings and access to suitable investment and protection services as those products and partners are approved and activated.

Can I register on this website?

New-customer registration is phone-first in the OpFin App. Web sign-in is intended for existing customers and authorised workspace users.

Are every savings, investment and insurance product shown here already live?

No. Product availability depends on eligibility, regulatory requirements, approved providers and activation. OpFin should not present a planned or configured capability as a live service.

Do employers or groups see my private personal finances?

OpFin Financial Spaces are designed to separate authority and privacy. Membership in a group or organisation does not by itself grant access to a person’s private Personal Space.
