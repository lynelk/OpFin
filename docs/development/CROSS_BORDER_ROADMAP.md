## Work packages before any foreign activation

1. **Product and partnerships:** choose one country/product/customer segment using evidenced demand, lender coverage, costs and partner readiness. No market-size or profitability figures are fabricated.
2. **Legal and operations:** produce the country responsibility map for lender, OpFin, distributor and payment provider; address data processing/transfers, tax, complaints, collections and reporting. Confirm source dates and distinguish drafts from in-force requirements.
3. **Backend:** replace the single runtime payment-currency boundary with certified per-country/currency routes; partition accounts and money instructions; add identity, bureau, tax and reporting adapters and local affordability policy.
4. **Clients and distribution:** localise language, currency formatting/minor units and disclosures; verify channel/build identity and territory-specific store policies. Package evidence for Google, Apple and Huawei without changing product definitions.
5. **Acceptance and release:** country fixtures, lender separation, exact-money/reversal/reconciliation tests, full UAT and a small activated segment with measurable rollback criteria. Existing obligations remain serviceable if new origination is paused.

## Continuous improvement workflow

When a management discussion or industry finding identifies an improvement, record its source/date, affected market/product, user need, evidence quality and intended outcome in the development backlog. Product and engineering turn the accepted change into a scoped implementation and acceptance criteria; operations/legal provide the relevant configuration and evidence. Link the implementation PR, test results and release decision. A review finding does not automatically change policy or create a reminder. This workflow supports national, regional and continental research without treating one country's rules as a universal product limit.
