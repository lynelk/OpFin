# Institutional ECL provisioning: legal ownership, source controls and accounting

Status: **development candidate, NOT production financial acceptance**  
Reviewed source branch: `feat/institutional-ecl-ownership-20261015`  
Document date: 15 October 2026  
Language: English (United Kingdom)

## Product and accounting boundary

This is an **extension** of the canonical OpFin credit, impairment and ledger architecture, not a new lending core. It introduces institution-specific governance, read-only external portfolio observations, forward-looking scenario estimates and signed reporting. An institution's legal lender identity, economic risk owner, accounting reporting entity and funding source must not be conflated.

| Exposure type | OpFin-managed loan | Institution-issued external loan | Provision result | Posting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `opfin` | yes, with independently attested legal risk owner | no | Full ECL | Existing `LoanImpairmentService` debit impairment expense / credit allowance after independent approval; product and currency accounts |
| `partner` | yes, when OpFin services the facility | yes, via controlled CSV or single observation | Full institution-specific ECL | **Advisory only** in OpFin. No partner expense/allowance in OpFin's ledger |
| `shared` | yes, with contract and risk share | not yet supported in external intake | Full ECL, displayed platform economic-risk share | **Advisory only until receivable/guarantee accounting is certified** |

An `opfin` classification means the OpFin legal reporting entity bears the actual credit risk. It cannot be inferred from application branding, capital management, disbursement execution, use of a wallet, service fees or collection activity. An institutional lender remains responsible for its own booked ECL unless the actual contractual structure changes that conclusion.

**Critical pre-release gap:** Existing platform loan disbursement accounting can book a full loan receivable without checking approved legal risk ownership before issuance. This feature rejects inappropriate *new impairment/write-off entries* for attested partner/shared loans but does **not** retrospectively remove underlying principal receivables, repair historic posting, or establish the shared-claim GL. The Accounting and Finance sign-off described below must resolve this before production activation.

## Source implementation

- `InstitutionalEclCalculator`: approved, scenario-weighted PD × LGD × gross outstanding calculation and stage assignment; exact integer minor-unit outputs.
- `InstitutionalEclService`: approved effective-dated policy, immutable loan risk-owner attestation, reviewed external source snapshots, transactional CSV batches, closed-source controls, draft/approved/posted/finalised runs, monthly movement reconciliation.
- `LoanImpairmentService`: unchanged canonical booked-loan assessment and GL adjustments, now explicitly rejects partner/shared or unapproved ownership when attested or governed by a live institutional policy.
- `CreditWriteOffService`: same accounting-owner guard prevents writing off third-party receivables against OpFin's allowance.
- `FinancialIntegrityService`: recognises separately attested partner/shared advisory exposure, flags missing reports, historic ledger contamination, unowned exposures and uncertified shared-credit accounting.
- API/controller, admin page, authenticated CSV downloads and feature tests.
- Protected database evidence: policy assumptions, legal owner attestation, source balances, batch manifests, ECL inputs and output amounts cannot be silently rewritten; authorised approval and posting transitions remain possible.

### Input data and source evidence

Canonical external CSV header (UTF-8, no monetary decimal values):

```csv
external_loan_reference,principal_outstanding_minor,days_past_due,sicr,credit_impaired,contractual_maturity_date
TEST-LOAN-001,500000,35,1,0,2027-06-30
TEST-LOAN-002,0,0,0,0,2027-06-30
```

The example is synthetic, not a deployed institution's loan book. `sicr` indicates independently established significant increase in credit risk; `credit_impaired` identifies documented credit impairment. Supply 1/0, not a subjective inference generated by OpFin.

A batch is limited to 2,000 loans and 2 MiB. The uploader supplies institution, as-of date, currency, MIS/authority/source reference, declared loan count and declared total outstanding principal. OpFin rejects incorrect totals and duplicated references and stages source evidence atomically. An independent platform administrator must approve before the file can be used in ECL.

Complete lender portfolios can span multiple reviewed batches, provided the monthly source counts and totals reconcile across all submissions. The same external loan reference/date cannot be silently overwritten. Include an explicit zero outstanding observation for a facility that closes; omissions are not silently interpreted as repayment. Historic corrections require an approved versioned source correction process, which is **not included in this candidate**.

The source observation contains no person names or identification numbers. Legal permission, contractual data-sharing terms, access restrictions and retention controls remain necessary for real institutional data. No file import becomes a new disbursement, application, borrower account or OpFin loan asset.

## Policy, stages and model governance

The institution selects, documents and independently approves a policy with:

- version, effective date and ISO currency;
- Stage 2 and Stage 3 DPD backstops;
- **two to five** forward-looking scenarios with exactly 10,000 basis points of probability weights;
- scenario-specific twelve-month default-event PD, lifetime default-event PD, credit-impaired PD and LGD, all in basis points;
- approved discount-rate proxy (basis points), methodology/calibration evidence reference;
- maker and different checker.

DPD backstops are not a substitute for qualitative credit deterioration, collateral values, accurate amortised-cost exposure, cure periods, lifetime cash-flow discounting, model validation, macroeconomic calibration and supervisory judgement. The current ECL is a **simplified scenario-weighted analytical estimate**, not a certified IFRS 9 engine. Stage 1 uses 12-month *default-event* PD and the discount period follows the remaining contractual term proxy; Stage 2 and 3 use lifetime/impaired PD. The simplified model must be validated against an institution's IFRS 9 accounting policy and independently approved data.

Reference reading: IFRS 9 paragraphs 5.5, IFRS Foundation forward-looking scenario guidance; Basel Committee/BIS guidance on the ECL three-stage model.

## Reporting, approvals and month-on-month movement

At a reporting cut-off:

1. A configured lender and approved policy are resolved for the reporting date.
2. Every OpFin-managed eligible loan must have approved credit-risk ownership, or the run fails closed.
3. Externally issued loans must come from reviewed snapshots. Declared full-source count, principal amount and coverage evidence must reconcile to the reviewed import set; any staged record blocks the run.
4. Managed-loan balances and DPD are read from the canonical repayment schedules. A historical cut-off is **not** reconstructed from today's mutable schedules. Capture and freeze on the actual cut-off day until historical subledger replay is independently implemented.
5. Snapshot each individual loan's gross outstanding, DPD, stage, model evidence, ECL and platform risk share. Store a tamper-evident calculation hash with a portfolio total.
6. Compare with the previous signed-off run; report each continuing facility's ECL movement, plus the institution's gross opening-to-closing ECL delta. Require explicit zero-balance rows for removed external references.
7. A second authorised administrator approves the run. Only platform-owned and correctly attested managed loans call the existing impairment/ledger service. Institution-owned portfolios are **finalised as advisory reports**, not marked as OpFin GL postings.
8. Download loan-level and portfolio-level CSV from the authenticated web portal. The report includes an institution ID, period, revision, classification and reconciliation reference.

**Period-end accounting caveat:** If approvals occur after the reporting cut-off, `ProductionLedgerService` books at actual posting time and respects period-close controls. Financial management must assess month-end accrual/cut-off treatment; the feature must not backdate the ledger or claim that a delayed transaction was posted in the prior accounting period.

## API and operating workflow

Administrative endpoints, all under authenticated `/api/admin/institutional-ecl`:

| Operation | Method / path |
| --- | --- |
| List policies, attestations, batches, observations, runs | `GET /?institution_id=...` |
| Stage institution model policy / approve separately | `POST /policies`; `POST /policies/{id}/approve` |
| Attest managed-loan ownership / approve separately | `POST /ownerships`; `POST /ownerships/{id}/approve` |
| Stage single external observation / approve separately | `POST /external-snapshots`; `POST /external-snapshots/{id}/approve` |
| Stage controlled CSV batch / approve independently | `POST /external-csv` multipart; `POST /import-batches/{id}/approve` |
| Build a calculated draft monthly statement | `POST /runs` with `institution_id`, `as_of_date`, `expected_external_count`, `expected_external_principal_minor`, `coverage_reference` |
| Approve or finalise/post approved report | `POST /runs/{id}/approve`; `POST /runs/{id}/post` |
| Read signed details, loan CSV, portfolio CSV | `GET /runs/{id}`; `GET /runs/{id}/export/loans`; `GET /runs/{id}/export/portfolio` |

The web workspace is `/admin/institutional-ecl`, linked from `/admin/lending-platform`. Ordinary programme users, borrowers and lenders do not receive generic access. Operations users need existing, controlled platform-credit delegation; independent financial approvals require a platform administrator who did not create the record.

## October 2026 accounting-completion candidate

The current PR additionally includes:

1. **Pre-acceptance attestation** in `credit_offer_ecl_ownerships`. Credit-risk ownership is contractually designated on the offered credit facility, reviewed by an independent checker, and copied to the immutable loan-level ownership once the provider confirms finality. All non-testing environments enforce this gate. A partner funding-pool mandate by itself never proves OpFin owns the credit risk.
2. **Verified cash reservation** in `partner_ecl_cash_reservations`. Partner-funded disbursements cannot proceed merely because a partner has committed capital. There must be a **pre-existing positive, reconciled fiduciary liability** in `liability.partner_funds_held.institution_{institution_id}`, in the offer currency. Concurrent pending payouts reserve that balance before payment instructions are submitted.
3. **Non-owned agency ledger**: a partner-owned successful payout *debits* funds held and *credits* provider cash, with no OpFin loan receivable, interest or customer-fee asset. Partner collections *debit* provider cash and *credit* funds held, with no OpFin interest/fee revenue. Reversals invert the original immutable custody entries.
4. **Ledger invariant**: `ProductionLedgerService` blocks any partner loan entry outside the balanced, two-line cash/provider and partner-funds-held structure. Account locking blocks any negative custody-funds liability. Ineligible shared-risk postings are rejected.
5. **Income/impairment guards**: partner loan fee-recognition accounting is suppressed; contractual late-interest exposure may be calculated for borrower servicing but is not posted as OpFin income. Platform impairment and write-offs are limited to valid platform-owned loans.
6. **Source corrections without erasure**: approved lender observations remain immutable. A maker proposes a correction, a different checker approves it, and a new advisory portfolio revision may replace the previous signed-off version. The old report is marked `superseded` but retained. The new report discloses its prior-run ID, reason and total ECL revision amount. Partner advisory corrections do not create or backdate platform GL entries.
7. **Access restrictions**: delegated operations staff may access only their own institution's calculations and source records. Independent finance approval remains platform-administrator only.
8. **Matched institutional prefunding journal**: `institution_ecl_funding_receipts` locks an independently reconciled and statement-matched institutional provider collection to one immutable source identity; the approved journal debits the provider disbursement cash account and credits institution-specific funds held. The source is marked accounted for inside the same transaction. Double posting or reusing customer loan collections is forbidden. A dedicated treasurer approval endpoint and admin interface are included.
9. **Added test code**: matched cash intake, accounting custody flows, reversal direction, negative fiduciary balance prevention, external source corrections/restatement, maker-checker and cross-institution access.

### Worked accounting illustrations

For an independent lender with **UGX 100,000 confirmed funds already held by OpFin** and an authorised, fully partner-owned UGX 60,000 cash disbursement:

| Event | Debit (UGX) | Credit (UGX) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Authorised payout (after cash reservation) | Partner funds held liability 60,000 | Provider cash 60,000 |
| Borrower repayment of principal and interest, total 20,000 | Provider cash 20,000 | Partner funds held liability 20,000 |
| Provider-confirmed payout reversal, if applicable | Provider cash 60,000 | Partner funds held liability 60,000 |

The lender's loan principal, contractual interest and credit-loss allowance remain on the lender's own accounting records. Collection service fee income for OpFin, if contractually earned, requires a separately approved service invoice/recognition event and must not be inferred from borrower repayments.

For an OpFin-owned loan, the canonical loan-principal asset, expected loss allowance and stage-3 write-off accounting continue to use existing `ProductionLoanLedgerService`, `LoanImpairmentService` and `CreditWriteOffService`; they are not duplicated in this extension.

### Actual deployment and acceptance constraints

**These are release blockers, not things confirmed as finished by the existence of code:**

- **Independent custody funding evidence:** An ECL treasury-intake code path now stages existing provider money movements only where the transaction is marked for institutional prefunding, is successful, reconciliation-matched, statement-matched, has no linked loan or offer and is not yet accounted for. A second treasury checker posts matching provider cash and partner funds held. This has **not** been exercised against a real bank/Cito provider statement; only actual matched receipts can unlock partner payouts. Capital-mandate fields, test journals and typed references are not proof of available money.
- **Legacy exposure remediation:** Any old partner-owned facility posted as an OpFin asset needs loan-by-loan contractual identification, dated GL/subledger reconciliation and auditor-reviewed immutable reclassification or reversal. The integrity monitor flags contamination but cannot invent or approve these journal corrections.
- **Shared participation/guarantee:** This must be separately classified as a loan asset participation, guarantee or other commitment under the executed agreement. Its booked amount, funding source, EAD, allowance, revenue allocations and capital treatment must be approved. It remains blocked for live origination; an advisory economic-loss share is not an on-balance-sheet journal.
- **IFRS 9 and prudential compliance:** Scenario-weighted PD × LGD × principal is an analytical estimate. Institution-specific historic default calibration, EAD (including accrued interest/commitments), discounted contractual cash shortfalls, recoveries, EIR, quantitative/qualitative SICR, stage-3 interest and policy backtesting have **not** been demonstrated. Do not represent the current calculator as audited IFRS 9-compliant.
- **Month-end and corrective journals:** Signed institutional advisory reports can be restated with append-only evidence. Posted OpFin-owned ECL is not retroactively overwritten. Any approved true-up must be booked in a *currently open period* and must explicitly identify the superseded period and the accounting authority.
- **Runtime evidence (17 October 2026):** An isolated checkout and disposable containers executed the entire SQLite Laravel suite (**620 passed / 23,264 assertions**), supported Node 22 Web typecheck, lint, build and **68 unit tests**, native PHP lint, PostgreSQL 16 ECL+finance tests (**17 passed / 92 assertions**) and **two consecutive successful PostgreSQL ECL schema-reset runs** (**7 passed / 41 assertions each**). Review `docs/verification/INSTITUTIONAL_ECL_FINANCE_ACCEPTANCE.md` for complete provenance. Live provider-bank reconciliation, independently validated contractual classification, model/calibration reviews, concurrent PostgreSQL stress tests, institutional security UAT and Finance/Risk sign-off remain **outstanding**. GitHub Actions stayed disabled; nothing was merged or deployed.
