# OpFin user manual

Status: User guidance with explicit capability boundaries  
Version: 21 October 2026  
Language: English (United Kingdom)

## What is available in the current source

OpFin's personal financial life includes Today/Home, aspirations, learning, planning and financial records. Financial Spaces extend participation to authorised groups and organisations. Source implementations also include governed credit, club accounting and treasury, financial-intelligence/statement review, purpose-bound Essentials, and role-specific organisational workflows. A visible action remains subject to its lender, institution, provider, entitlement and release gates. See the [current source/evidence register](../operations/DOCUMENTATION_ALIGNMENT_2026-10-21.md).

Use **Saved requests** to recover an interrupted club accounting instruction or statement request. Read its original result before acknowledging it; acknowledgement is not independent financial approval. For an uncertain collection, do not attempt another payment with a new key merely because the first result is delayed.

For issuer statements, **analysis is not authentication**. An issuer name, PDF or CSV can be unverified, quarantined, stale or subject to appeal. A financial-health recommendation is not a credit assessment. The [current capability supplement](CURRENT_CAPABILITY_SUPPLEMENT.md) explains the limits.

## What OpFin is

OpFin helps you understand, manage, plan and improve your money. One identity can give you access to your private Personal Space and, when invited or authorised, households, savings groups, businesses, SACCOs and partner/programme contexts.

This guide describes the current source workflows. A visible feature does not by itself establish live service availability. See the [current capability supplement](CURRENT_CAPABILITY_SUPPLEMENT.md) for treasury and Essentials limitations and the [current-state record](../CURRENT_STATE.md) for release status.

## Start and sign in

New customers start in the App with phone verification, OTP, names and a six-digit PIN. OpFin creates the Personal Space and asks for additional verification when the selected activity requires it. A second phone is optional.

The website provides information and Web access for existing or authorised users; its password-compatible sign-in is not the preferred new-customer App registration journey. Never give another person your PIN or OTP.

## Home and Financial Spaces

Home presents your financial position and next action. Use **My money & spaces** to switch context. Confirm the Space name before recording, approving or sharing information. Joining a group, employer or programme must not automatically expose your Personal Space.

## Money and planning

Record accounts, money in/out, budgets and upcoming commitments. Safe-to-spend depends on the information actually recorded and is guidance, not guaranteed future cash.

Add assets under **I own**, debts/payables under **I owe**, and receivables under **Owed to me**. Keep amounts and currencies accurate. A recorded asset is not necessarily liquid or independently valued.

## Savings groups and investment-club treasury

Create or join the authorised Savings Group context through **My money & spaces**. Officials may act only within their assigned roles; members' personal finances do not become group records.

Current club treasury adds account records, receipts/payments, CSV statement import, reconciliation review and issued statements. The detailed import/reconciliation workflow is currently a Web administration task. App statement access does not mean every club administration action is available on mobile.

Before a treasury task, verify the group, account, currency, opening balance and opening date. Keep statement dates as supplied by the source. Review suggested matches and explain genuine differences before confirmation. Do not alter dates or add an artificial balancing entry to bypass an error.

A generated OpFin statement reflects recorded/reviewed information; it is not a bank-issued statement. Separate currencies must stay separate unless a supported, evidenced conversion is explicitly provided. Treasury does not yet establish a complete member-capital, unitisation/NAV, distributions or investment-performance system.

Historical-date regression failures currently affect acceptance of treasury workflows. An authorised operator should investigate an opening-baseline error rather than asking you to change accurate source records. The [task supplement](CURRENT_CAPABILITY_SUPPLEMENT.md) describes controlled training and review.

## Businesses, employers, SACCOs and partners

Organisation users complete progressive institutional verification and use Workspaces for deeper administration. Employer services are capabilities of a Business Space rather than a duplicate employer identity. Organisation membership does not provide automatic access to private personal information.

## Save, invest, protect and borrow

Use only services confirmed as available to you. Regulated services may require identity, consent, eligibility, suitability, partner activation and other checks. Review the provider, costs, disclosures, due dates and obligations before confirming.

Submitting a provider request does not prove money moved. Pending, failed and completed states remain distinct until supported by provider evidence and reconciliation. Do not create a second money-changing request merely because the first response was delayed.

## Responsible credit

Where credit is available, review the full offer before acceptance. A profile limit is not guaranteed approval; affordability, identity, consent and product policy still apply. Multiple phones, wallets or lender offers must not multiply overall headroom.

Current positive-only employment enrichment may provide a capped benefit under an approved policy. Missing, unavailable or negative information is neutral under that enrichment rule. It is not permission to use unrelated private workplace information.

## Financial resilience

Open **Financial resilience** for financial-health guidance, a financial-reputation stage and practical next steps. Neither the reputation stage nor financial-health status is a substitute credit score, loan approval, price or limit.

Where **Use recorded data** is available, it creates a wellbeing snapshot from existing OpFin evidence. Missing information remains missing.

## Inclusive-finance programmes

Programme participation is separate from credit decisioning. Participant measurements require the applicable active measurement consent and enrolment. Questions may be delivered through App, Web, verified WhatsApp, USSD or authorised assisted capture when those channels are configured.

Reviewed translations are used where available; otherwise English fallback is explicit. Programme eligibility is not a credit decision. Voluntary demographic, disability, displacement and similar programme fields do not become hidden credit-risk inputs.

Leaving a programme closes its participation window for later measurement while preserving legitimate earlier evidence. Programme partners receive aggregate, programme-scoped reports, not unrestricted individual records. Measured change is not automatically proof of programme causality.

## Alternative credit support

A supported salary undertaking, guarantee, receivable or warehouse receipt follows **submitted → independently verified/rejected → recognised by applicable product policy**. Submission or verification alone does not approve credit.

## OpFin Essentials

Essentials is the source capability for purpose-bound electricity, water, connectivity, household-energy and rent finance. The offer must identify the participating third-party lender; OpFin arranges the journey and servicing rather than being described as the primary Essentials lender.

The intended flow is service/rental-beneficiary capture and verification, eligibility, amount selection, lender/cost/term review, explicit acceptance, direct provider/beneficiary settlement and repayment tracking. It is not unrestricted wallet cash-out. Connected platforms such as Stolets require customer permissions, and those permissions can be reviewed or revoked.

**Live financial activation of the reviewed Essentials implementation has not been accepted.** Internal control findings and external provider requirements remain. Do not use these instructions as authorisation for a real test loan or payout. A pending lender request, provider fulfilment or repayment must not be described as complete before confirmation.

## Location and accessibility

Location is optional for baseline financial use. Where a particular location-based task is supported, review its stated purpose and permission; use a manual fallback where available. Do not infer continuous tracking or a location-driven credit decision from service discovery.

Use supported larger-text, simple-wording, reduced-motion and high-contrast preferences. Physical-device acceptance is still required for certification-level assistive-technology claims. Assisted verification must not involve sharing a PIN/OTP or lowering identity assurance.

## Offline, support and privacy

Supported offline capture can queue non-final actions for synchronisation. Financial actions still require server/provider confirmation.

Use Support for a problem or complaint and retain the case/reference. Review permissions and consents in the account experience. The website provides privacy-policy and deletion access. Financial, regulatory, fraud, audit and dispute records may require lawful retention after account closure.

Open financial obligations must be handled through governed closure. The reviewed Essentials deletion interaction requires further control acceptance; do not assume an active advance can be safely removed merely because account deletion is available.

## Availability

Internal software defects, missing acceptance evidence and external provider/legal activation are different limitations. None should be represented as completed without evidence. A published manual, source merge or successful deployment of one component does not establish that every service is live.


## Club requests and interrupted work

If a club instruction or statement request times out, open **Saved requests** and inspect/resume the original request. Do not create a replacement just because the result is uncertain. Read the recorded result and acknowledge it before starting another request of the same purpose. Cancellation is available only if the request was not submitted to the accounting domain. Acknowledgement is not accounting approval or payment execution. Former-member capital history is limited to the former member's own retained records. Opening a mobile save/share/print dialogue is not proof that a file was delivered or printed.

## Essentials repayment status

A repayment can remain pending while the provider confirms the outcome. An uncertain result must be reconciled against the original request, not resolved by submitting a second payment. Reserved financing is not an amount currently due. Availability of any Essentials product depends on verified lender authority, product configuration and provider capability; this guide does not claim that every lender, biller, repayment route or refund path is live.

## When external financial actions are unavailable

If a lender, biller, issuer, savings provider, insurer or payment route is not activated for your country and product, continue using lawful record-keeping, planning, learning and support features where available. Never treat a suggested provider as an activated agreement or infer settlement from an initial response. Do not submit genuine funds to test a feature described only as source-defined.
