# OpFin User Manual

Version: 20 September 2026

## What OpFin is
OpFin helps you understand, manage, plan and improve your money. One sign-in can give you access to your private Personal Space and, when invited or authorised, households, savings groups, businesses, SACCOs and other organisations.

## Start and sign in
Register with your phone, OTP, name and PIN. OpFin creates your personal financial context. Complete additional identity information only when the service you choose requires it. Never give another person your PIN.

## Home
Home shows the clearest next action and your financial position. Use **My money & spaces** to switch context. Your Personal Space remains private when you join a group or employer.

## Money and planning
Record accounts, money in/out, budgets and upcoming commitments. Safe-to-spend is money recorded as available less relevant upcoming commitments; it is guidance, not a guarantee of future cash.

## What I own, owe and am owed
In a Financial Space, add assets under **I own**, debts/payables under **I owe**, and receivables under **Owed to me**. OpFin uses these records to build a clearer financial position/net-worth view.

## Savings groups
Create a Savings Group from **My spaces → Add or join**. Invite members by phone, manage the group context and record group financial information in the App. A member's personal finances do not become visible to the group.

## Businesses, SACCOs and partners
Create the organisation Space and complete progressive institutional verification. Web Workspace provides deeper administration. Employer services are enabled on a Business Space rather than creating a duplicate employer identity.

## Save, invest, insure and borrow
Use only products shown as available to you. Regulated products may require identity, consent, eligibility or suitability checks. Review costs, disclosures and obligations before confirming. Partner availability can vary.

## Offline and low-connectivity use
Where the App supports offline capture, queued changes remain visible and synchronise when connectivity returns. Money-changing actions require provider confirmation and cannot be assumed complete merely because they were initiated offline.

## Accessibility
Use clear visual actions and accessibility preferences where available. OpFin is designed to minimise typing and progressively disclose detail. Assisted onboarding must never require surrendering your PIN.

## Help and privacy
Use Support for a problem or complaint. Permissions and consents can be reviewed in the account experience. Organisation membership never grants automatic access to private Personal Space data.
