# OpFin User Manual

Status: Public user guidance  
Version: 23 September 2026  
Language: English (United Kingdom)

## What OpFin is

OpFin helps you understand, manage, plan and improve your money. One identity can give you access to your private Personal Space and, when invited or authorised, households, savings groups, businesses, SACCOs and partner/programme spaces.

## Start and sign in

New customers start in the OpFin App with phone verification, OTP, names and a six-digit PIN. OpFin then creates the Personal Space and asks for additional verification only when the chosen activity requires it.

The public website is an information and Web-access surface. Its sign-in route is for existing or authorised users and is not the preferred new-customer registration path.

Never give another person your PIN or OTP.

## Home and Financial Spaces

Home shows the clearest next action and your financial position. Use **My money & spaces** to switch context. A Personal Space remains private when you join a group, employer or programme.

## Money and planning

Record accounts, money in/out, budgets and upcoming commitments. Safe-to-spend guidance depends on the information actually recorded. It is not a guarantee of future cash.

## What I own, owe and am owed

In a Financial Space, add assets under **I own**, debts/payables under **I owe**, and receivables under **Owed to me**. OpFin uses these records to build a clearer financial-position/net-worth view.

## Savings groups

Create or join a Savings Group from **My money & spaces**. Invite members, review membership and manage supported group financial information in the App. A member's personal finances do not become visible to the group.

## Businesses, employers, SACCOs and partners

Organisation users complete progressive institutional verification and use Web Workspaces for deeper administration. Employer services are capabilities of a Business Space rather than a second employer identity.

## Save, invest, protect and borrow

Use only services shown as available to you. Regulated services may require identity, consent, eligibility, suitability, partner activation or other checks. Review costs, disclosures, due dates and obligations before confirming.

A request to a provider is not proof that money moved. Pending, failed and completed states remain distinct until provider evidence and reconciliation support the final outcome.

## Responsible credit

Where credit is available, OpFin shows the relevant offer, cost and repayment information before acceptance. A profile limit is not a promise of approval. Affordability, identity, consent and product rules still apply.

Verified positive employment behaviour may support an approved policy where configured. Missing, unavailable or negative employment-behaviour information is neutral under the current enrichment approach.

## Financial resilience

Open **Financial resilience** to view financial-health guidance, your financial-reputation stage and practical next steps.

The financial-reputation stage is not another credit score. Financial-health status is also not a credit score and does not itself approve credit, change a limit or change pricing.

Where **Use recorded data** is available, OpFin can build a health snapshot from information already held in your Financial Space. Missing information remains missing.

## Inclusive-finance programmes

Programme participation is separate from credit decisioning.

If you voluntarily join an inclusive-finance programme:

- programme-measurement consent must be active for participant measurements;
- programme questions may be delivered through App, Web, verified WhatsApp, USSD or authorised assisted capture;
- reviewed translations are used where configured; otherwise English fallback is explicit;
- programme eligibility/ineligibility is not a credit approval/decline;
- voluntary demographic, disability, displacement and similar programme fields do not become hidden risk inputs;
- you can leave a programme; later activity is not silently counted inside the closed participation period.

Programme partners receive aggregate, programme-scoped reporting. They do not receive unrestricted individual participant records.

## Alternative credit-support evidence

Where supported, you may submit evidence such as a salary undertaking, guarantee, receivable or warehouse receipt. The lifecycle is:

**submitted → independently verified/rejected → recognised by an applicable product policy**

Submission or verification alone does not approve credit.

## Accessibility and assisted use

Use supported preferences for larger text, simple wording, reduced movement and high contrast. Platform screen-reader support still requires physical-device acceptance before certification-level claims.

Assisted onboarding must never require surrendering a PIN or OTP.

## Offline and low-connectivity use

Supported offline capture may queue non-final actions for synchronisation. Money-changing actions still need server/provider confirmation. Do not assume a financial action completed merely because it was initiated before connectivity dropped.

## Support, privacy and account deletion

Use Support for a problem or complaint. Review permissions and consents through the account experience.

The website provides a public privacy-policy link and an account-deletion path. Account deletion may retain records that must lawfully or operationally remain for financial, regulatory, fraud, audit or dispute obligations.

Organisation or programme membership never grants automatic access to private Personal Space data.

## What remains externally dependent

The app may contain code for a service that is not yet activated. Provider credentials, partner contracts, regulatory approvals, app-store publication, programme agreements, reviewed translations and physical-device certification are real external gates. OpFin should show an unavailable/pending state rather than pretending these gates have been completed.
