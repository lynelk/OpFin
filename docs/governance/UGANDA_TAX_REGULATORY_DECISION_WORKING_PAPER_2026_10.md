# OpFin Uganda regulatory and tax decision memorandum
24 October 2026 | Management working paper, not a legal opinion

## Confirmed management instructions
Six risk factors: macroeconomic, employment, geographic, transactions, concentration and fraud. All new risk factors remain shadow-only pending Uganda outcome validation, independent review and formal policy approval. Existing tax settings remain unchanged while reviewed. Evaluate BOTH investor structures: (A) OpFin lender of record with investor funding and (B) investors as direct lenders.

## Questions for Uganda-qualified counsel and tax advisers
1. Determine the actual licensed entity and permitted activities under applicable Uganda money-lending, Tier IV, banking, payments and capital-markets law. Confirm if either investor structure requires further approval, registration, licensing, custody or client-money safeguards.
2. Analyse the legal status of interest, access/service fees, disbursement charges, late fees and insurance charges for each OpFin product. Distinguish financing income from separately taxable services; confirm VAT treatment with citations and any URA interpretive uncertainty.
3. Confirm income tax recognition, withholding agents and rates by payment direction and tax residence, bad debt provisioning/write-offs, recoveries and investor distributions. Provide worked UGX examples for both investor structures.
4. Confirm EFRIS and invoice/credit-note obligations, tax return schedules, and ledger treatment. Validate against actual contracts, tax registrations and historic filings before configuration changes.
5. Review consent, privacy, fair lending, explainability, adverse action, data retention and access for alternative data and shadow scoring; confirm permissible purposes and regulator notification or approval needs.
6. Confirm whether climate/geographic, employment, economic, fraud and concentration signals introduce protected-class proxies, discrimination or consumer protection concerns and what validation and appeal rights are necessary.

## Evidence and approval gate
Collect entity registration, TIN, regulatory licences, current product terms, sample customer and investor contracts, flow-of-funds diagrams, fee schedules, tax registrations/returns, invoices, GL mappings, reconciliation evidence and URA/regulator correspondence.

Management must select the legally feasible investor structure after counsel's written comparative opinion. Finance and compliance must sign off tax mappings. Risk and compliance must approve shadow-model promotion only after Uganda cohort validation, bias testing, drift thresholds, auditability and rollback testing. Engineering must verify CI, reconciliation integrity, consent revocation and staging deployment before production.

## Explicit non-conclusions
No Ugandan statutory tax rate, exemption, licence or legal status is asserted by this working paper. It is not a signed professional opinion. No tax, lending or investment configuration change is authorised by it.
