# OpFin Integrated Management System Policy

Status: Controlled internal policy proposal; adoption not yet evidenced  
Prepared: 25 September 2026  
Language: English (United Kingdom)

## Purpose

OpFin shall operate one evidence-led integrated management system for quality, information security, privacy, service management and business continuity, with supporting financial-message, software-quality, accessibility, AI and payment-security requirements applied according to scope.

Publication of this proposal in the repository is not organisational adoption, certification or evidence of operating effectiveness. IMS-01 in the [action register](ISO_READINESS_ACTION_REGISTER.md) records the approval evidence required before the policy becomes effective.

## Principles

1. Customer and regulatory needs shall become traceable requirements before implementation.
2. Leadership shall be accountable for quality, ethical conduct, customer outcomes and adequate resources.
3. Financial correctness, privacy, security, accessibility and continuity shall be acceptance criteria, not post-release activities.
4. Risks and opportunities shall be assessed separately, assigned owners and reviewed for effectiveness.
5. Material changes shall follow requirement, regulatory classification, architecture, UX, security/privacy, continuity/service impact, financial-control review, build, test, operational acceptance, release, measurement and corrective action.
6. External-provider acknowledgement is not financial finality. Financial state shall remain governed by authoritative provider outcome, ledger and reconciliation controls.
7. No unavailable provider, regulatory, licensing or certification fact may be fabricated.
8. Suppliers and subprocessors shall be assessed according to the service/data risk they introduce.
9. Incidents, complaints, defects and nonconformities shall be recorded, analysed and used for corrective action and improvement. Establishment and demonstrated effectiveness of this process remain subject to IMS-10; this policy does not assert that they already operate.
10. Evidence shall identify its subject, scope, owner, reviewer and date. Software release evidence shall additionally identify the exact candidate and environment. Those fields are not required for an unrelated organisational record; mark them not applicable rather than inventing metadata. Historical evidence shall not be silently restamped.
11. GitHub Actions shall remain disabled unless separately authorised. Equivalent candidate-specific verification must not be weakened.
12. Production or provider activation shall require the relevant legal, operational, security, financial and accessibility evidence for the exact scope.

## Management-system scope

The requested QMS target is ISO 9001:2026. The applicability register shall also assess ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27000, ISO/IEC 20000-1, ISO/IEC 27032, ISO 22301, ISO 20022, ISO 8583, ISO 9362 and ISO 32212. Edition, publication status, amendments and transition arrangements must be verified against the issuer and controlled/licensed standards before adoption or a conformity claim. This proposal is not an assertion that a particular revision has been published or adopted.

It shall additionally assess ISO/IEC 27701, ISO/IEC 27002, ISO/IEC 27017, ISO/IEC 27018, ISO/IEC 25010, ISO/IEC 42001, PCI DSS and WCAG/native accessibility requirements according to actual OpFin scope.

## Governance records

Maintain controlled registers for context and interested parties; legal/regulatory obligations; services, assets, data and dependencies; suppliers/subprocessors; risks, opportunities and impacts; controls/Statement of Applicability where relevant; competence and awareness; incidents/nonconformities/corrective action; complaints and feedback; internal audits; management reviews; continuity exercises; and continual improvement.

The [action register](ISO_READINESS_ACTION_REGISTER.md) records work state separately from evidence maturity. Find these documents through the [documentation hub](../README.md) and apply the [publication register](../PUBLICATION_REGISTER.md).

## Conformity claims

Repository implementation, documentation and tests can provide evidence of controls but cannot by themselves establish certification or operating effectiveness. External certification claims require a defined scope and verified evidence from an appropriately accredited certification body.
