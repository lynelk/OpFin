# OpFin Integrated Management System Policy

Status: Controlled internal policy  
Effective: 25 September 2026  
Language: English (United Kingdom)

## Purpose

OpFin shall operate one evidence-led integrated management system for quality, information security, privacy, service management and business continuity, with supporting financial-message, software-quality, accessibility, AI and payment-security requirements applied according to scope.

## Principles

1. Customer and regulatory needs become traceable requirements before implementation.
2. Leadership is accountable for quality, ethical conduct, customer outcomes and adequate resources.
3. Financial correctness, privacy, security, accessibility and continuity are acceptance criteria, not post-release decorations.
4. Risks and opportunities are assessed separately, assigned owners and reviewed for effectiveness.
5. Material changes follow requirement, regulatory classification, architecture, UX, security/privacy, continuity/service impact, financial-control review, build, test, operational acceptance, release, measurement and improvement/CAPA.
6. External-provider acknowledgement is not financial finality. Financial state remains governed by authoritative provider outcome, ledger and reconciliation controls.
7. No unavailable provider, regulatory, licensing or certification fact may be fabricated.
8. Suppliers and subprocessors are assessed according to the service/data risk they introduce.
9. Incidents, complaints, defects and nonconformities are recorded, analysed and used for corrective action and improvement.
10. Evidence must identify the exact candidate, environment, owner, reviewer and date. Historical evidence is not silently restamped.
11. GitHub Actions remain disabled unless separately authorised. Equivalent candidate-specific verification must not be weakened.
12. Production or provider activation requires the relevant legal, operational, security, financial and accessibility evidence for the exact scope.

## Management-system scope

The applicability register shall consider at least ISO 9001:2026, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 with applicable amendments, ISO/IEC 27000:2026, ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 with applicable amendments, ISO/IEC 27032:2023, ISO 22301:2019 with applicable amendments, ISO 20022, ISO 8583:2023, ISO 9362:2022 and ISO 32212:2026.

It shall additionally assess ISO/IEC 27701, ISO/IEC 27002, ISO/IEC 27017, ISO/IEC 27018, ISO/IEC 25010, ISO/IEC 42001, PCI DSS and WCAG/native accessibility requirements according to actual OpFin scope.

## Governance records

Maintain controlled registers for context and interested parties; legal/regulatory obligations; services, assets, data and dependencies; suppliers/subprocessors; risks, opportunities and impacts; controls/Statement of Applicability where relevant; competence and awareness; incidents/nonconformities/CAPA; complaints and feedback; internal audits; management reviews; continuity exercises; and continual improvement.

## Conformity claims

Repository implementation, documentation and tests can provide evidence of controls but cannot by themselves establish certification or operating effectiveness. External certification claims require the defined scope and evidence of an appropriately accredited certification body.
