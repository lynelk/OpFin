## Full description

OpFin brings everyday financial tools into one clear, secure experience.

Use OpFin to:

- understand your financial position;
- explore eligible credit options from responsible providers;
- review the amount received, interest, fees, equivalent APR, repayment schedule and total repayment before accepting an offer;
- track active applications and repayments;
- build savings goals and healthier money habits;
- request support or hardship assistance; and
- manage consent, privacy and account deletion.

Credit is subject to eligibility, affordability assessment, identity verification, provider approval and applicable law. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval or disbursement.

Personal-loan terms made available through the Google Play version require more than 60 days for full repayment. Standard mobile terms start at 90 days where an eligible product is available.

Before accepting an offer, you will see:

- the amount you receive;
- every applicable fee and interest charge;
- the equivalent maximum annual percentage rate (APR);
- the minimum and maximum repayment period;
- repayment dates and frequency; and
- the total amount repayable.

OpFin does not request access to your contacts, call history, SMS messages, photos or videos for personal-loan decisions.

Provider and regulatory availability may vary. Features that are not yet approved or connected remain unavailable.
