# OpFin Google Play listing pack

This folder is the controlled source for OpFin's first Google Play release. It covers package `co.opfin.app`, version `1.0.0 (17)`, initial distribution in Uganda, and the production Android channel `play_store`.

The pack does not assert that a licence, provider approval or production capability exists. Replace every bracketed evidence item with verified legal or operational evidence before submission.

## Contents

- `listing.md`: ready-to-paste English store copy.
- `financial-features.md`: declaration and evidence checklist for the Finance category.
- `data-safety.md`: data-inventory worksheet that must be reconciled with the production build and privacy policy.
- `reviewer-notes.md`: Play reviewer access and test path.
- `asset-manifest.md`: required artwork and screenshot plan.
- `release-checklist.md`: accountable submission sequence.
- `release-automation.md`: protected GitHub environment and signing setup.
- `assets/`: reproducible store icon and feature graphic generated from the canonical OpFin logo.

Do not upload an AAB until the API product catalogue contains an eligible term of at least 61 days. The Android route prefers a 90-day-or-longer term when one is available and rejects any selected term under 61 days.
