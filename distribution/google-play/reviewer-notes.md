The review account must contain verified test KYC and consent records, but no real person's identity or financial data.

## Review journey

1. Sign in using the supplied review account.
2. Open **Borrow** and choose **Check my options**.
3. Submit a test amount and purpose.
4. Open **Review my credit offers**.
5. Confirm the displayed offer has a duration of at least 61 days; the standard test offer should use 90 days.
6. Review amount received, interest, fees, equivalent APR, repayment frequency, total repayment and final due date.
7. Do not execute a real payout. The review tenant/provider must be a controlled non-money-moving environment clearly identified to the reviewer.
8. Open **More** → **Privacy & account** → **Delete account** to verify the in-app deletion route.

## Important context

- Approval and disbursement are not guaranteed.
- Provider- or regulator-gated capabilities remain unavailable until approved.
- The app does not request contacts, SMS, call logs, photos or video permissions for credit decisions.
