## Review journey

1. Sign in with the supplied phone and six-digit PIN.
2. On **Home**, verify that the account shows its current borrower state, including available limit or amount due.
3. Open **Borrow** → **Apply for a loan**.
4. Confirm the Loan Application page shows **Available loan limit** and **Amount due**.
5. Enter a test amount within the available limit, choose an eligible repayment period and purpose, then submit the request.
6. Open **Activity** → **Credit offers** if an offer is available.
7. Review amount received, interest, fees, equivalent APR where applicable, repayment period/frequency, total repayment and payment timing.
8. Confirm the offer separately asks for credit-information reporting consent and that the detailed pricing/default/complaints disclosure is visible.
9. Confirm that a verified payout wallet is selected before offer acceptance.
10. **Do not execute a real payout.** The reviewer environment must use an approved controlled path that cannot create an unauthorised financial obligation.
12. Open **More** → **Accessibility** and confirm larger-text/reduced-motion controls are present.
11. Open **More** → **Privacy & account** → **Delete account** to verify the in-app deletion route.

## KYC review path

If identity verification must be demonstrated, use authorised test identity evidence only. The flow requires:

1. NIN;
2. National ID front;
3. National ID back;
4. photo of the reviewer/test customer holding the ID.

The app requests camera access for this purpose. It does not require broad gallery/storage access.

## Important context

- A profile credit limit is not guaranteed approval.
- Missing mandatory affordability/provider data can refer a request for review.
- Pending provider payout/collection states are not described as completed money movement.
- Provider/regulator-gated capabilities remain unavailable until activated.
- OpFin never asks the reviewer/customer to send a PIN through WhatsApp or USSD.
