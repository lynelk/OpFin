# Google Play reviewer notes worksheet

Status: Controlled confidential release worksheet  
Updated: 23 September 2026  
Audience: release owner and Play Console administrator

Do not commit reviewer credentials to the repository. Complete the credential fields directly in Play Console from the approved release record.

## App access fields to complete in Play Console

- Reviewer phone/account: approved dedicated test account
- PIN: enter only in Play Console
- OTP path: approved reviewer-safe OTP instruction or controlled test number
- Country: Uganda

The review account must use authorised test identity/financial data and a controlled non-money-moving or specifically approved test-provider path.

## Review journey

1. Sign in with the supplied phone and six-digit PIN.
2. On **Home**, verify the account shows the appropriate current financial/credit state.
3. Open **Borrow** → **Apply for a loan** when credit is enabled for the reviewer account.
4. Confirm the application shows the available loan limit and amount due where applicable.
5. Enter a permitted test amount, choose an eligible repayment period and purpose, then submit the request.
6. Open **Activity** → **Credit offers** if an offer is available.
7. Review amount received, interest, fees, equivalent APR where applicable, repayment period/frequency, total repayment and payment timing.
8. Confirm the offer separately captures required credit-information reporting consent and exposes the applicable pricing/default/complaints disclosure.
9. Confirm a verified payout wallet is selected before offer acceptance.
10. **Do not execute an unauthorised real payout.** Use only the approved reviewer environment/path.
11. Open **More** → **Accessibility** and review supported accessibility controls.
12. Open **More** → **Privacy & account** → **Delete account** to verify the in-app deletion route.

## KYC review path

If identity verification must be demonstrated, use authorised test identity evidence only. The applicable journey may require NIN, National ID front/back and selfie/identity evidence according to the configured verification policy.

The app requests camera access for direct capture. It must not require broad gallery/storage access merely to perform personal-loan decisioning.

## Reviewer context

- A profile credit limit is not guaranteed approval.
- Missing mandatory affordability/provider data may cause referral/pending review.
- Pending provider payout/collection states are not completed money movement.
- Provider/regulator-gated capabilities remain unavailable until activated.
- Programme measurement/protected attributes remain outside underwriting.
- OpFin never asks a reviewer/customer to send a PIN through WhatsApp, USSD or support chat.
